## What it is

HS Tools is the stock-firmware card from Henry Skillz: stems on the device,
key and BPM tags in the browser, and the SEQ desk with LUFS meters. It does
**not** include the HS effects or track chains — those need SampQOL OS via
[HenrySkillz D.S.B](/mod/henryskillz-mod-kit/).

Hardware tested by the maker on 15 Nov 2026 (firmware 1.3.0.12).

## Install

1. Download `henryskillz-mpc-sample-hs-tools.zip` from the
   [v0.6.1 release](https://github.com/Holla-Back-Entertainment/henryskillz-mpc-sample-mod-kit/releases/tag/v0.6.1).
2. Copy its `autorun` folder to an exFAT SD card (rename any existing `autorun`
   out of the way first).
3. Power on the Sample.

Undo: switch off and take the card out.

Guide: [D.S.B Guide — HS Tools](https://www.henryskillz.com/dsb-guide).
