## What it is

A pre-release plug-and-play SD card from Henry Skillz for the MPC Sample on
**firmware 1.4.0.7 only**. The zip ships D.S.B, HS 25, HS Treble Bass and 16 HS
effects (official plugins). No SampQOL on this card — it does not work on 1.4.
Other makers’ OSes need their own cards.

## Install

1. Back up the SD card.
2. Download `HS-Plug-and-Play-FW1.4.zip` from
   [fw1.4-plug-and-play-v1](https://github.com/Holla-Back-Entertainment/henryskillz-mpc-sample-mod-kit/releases/tag/fw1.4-plug-and-play-v1).
3. Unzip to the card root and follow `README-INSTALL.txt`.

The maker notes that Developer Mode / Enhancements steps come from Akai’s SDK
docs and have not been confirmed on a real Sample; the launcher only runs on
exact 1.4.0.7.

Build log: [henryskillz.com/mpc-sample](https://www.henryskillz.com/mpc-sample).
