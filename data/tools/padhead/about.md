# Padhead

Free tools for the Akai MPC Sample that run in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; your sounds stay on your computer.

## The five tools

- **Kit maker (Pad Banks)** — Turn one sound into a playable bank of 16 pads in your key and scale, then export a project the Sample opens straight away.
- **Sound Lab** — Generate royalty-free sounds, shape them, and send them to pads in a scale or export as WAV.
- **Sample chop** — Load or record a sample; Padhead finds the tempo and loop, then chops it across 16 pads.
- **Trackouts / mixer** — Open a project from the Sample in the browser, edit hits, mix every track, then export stems or save it back to the card.
- **Arrange** — Take the tracks of an MPC sequence and build a full song with sections, filter sweeps and volume moves.

## Getting exports onto the Sample

Export and unzip the kit or project folder, copy it to the SD card (SHIFT + pad 16 → SD Card Access), then on the MPC use Load → the kit folder → `.xpj`.

Full tools list and guides: [padhead.com](https://padhead.com/).
