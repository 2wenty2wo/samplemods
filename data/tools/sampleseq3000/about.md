# SampleSeq3000

Free step sequencer that builds drum and melodic patterns in your browser and exports a project for the Akai MPC Sample. Everything runs locally; nothing is uploaded.

## Export to the Sample

Press **Export .xpj**, unzip the download, and copy both the `.xpj` and its `_[ProjectData]` folder into the same folder on the SD card. Projects have been confirmed loading on the MPC Sample.

## Preview vs device

The in-browser preview is an approximation using the template samples. The device applies its own engine, filters and envelopes, so the exported `.xpj` on the Sample is the source of truth.

Site and guides: [sampleseq3000.com](https://www.sampleseq3000.com/).
